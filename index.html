About SIU

A quiet campus of its own, in the middle of Sylhet city

Sylhet International University is a privately funded, non-profit institution of higher learning and research, established under the Private University Act 1992 and affiliated with the University Grants Commission of Bangladesh. Degrees are accepted for higher study and employment at home and abroad.

Admission Information Contact the University

SIU at a glance

  • Academically rigorous but practical instruction in professional disciplines.
  • Collaborative programmes with universities in the USA, Australia, Canada and the United Kingdom.
  • A campus of its own in a relatively calm and quiet part of the city.
  • Credits earned at SIU are accepted by universities at home and abroad.

Vision

The vision of Sylhet International University is to infuse into its students a passion for learning that will help them grow in their professional lives, and thereby contribute to building a better society of the 21st century.

Mission

The principal aim of SIU is to provide high-quality education at undergraduate and postgraduate levels relevant to the needs of a dynamic society. The mission is achieved by:

  • Providing an excellent teaching-learning environment
  • Professionally accomplished faculty with an appropriate balance of academic qualifications
  • Financial grants to poor but meritorious students
  • Education with ethical concern and social responsibility
  • Stimulating the search for knowledge and inquiry across disciplines
  • Creating creative, independent thinkers and leaders

Accommodation

Admission

How to get admitted

The admission office advises prospective students and guardians, and individual counselling on campus is encouraged — you are welcome to visit.

  1. 1

    Choose a programme

    Use the Find a Program filter — undergraduate or graduate — to see fees and waivers.

  2. 2

    Collect the form

    Get the admission form from the Admission Office or the campus office.

  3. 3

    Submit documents

    SSC and HSC transcripts or certificates, photographs, and diploma certificates if applicable.

  4. 4

    Pay the fees

    Admission fee and registration as per the published fee structure.

  5. 5

    Get confirmed

    Receive your admission confirmation and student ID, then complete orientation.

Eligibility

  • At least a 2.5 GPA in each of SSC and HSC from a board of university or equivalent examination.
  • Or a Diploma in Engineering from BTEB (or a BTEB-recognised diploma) with a GPA of 2.5 or above out of 4.0, for the B.Sc. Engineering programme.
  • International students must meet the same academic standards, plus English proficiency and certification of adequate financial resources.

Documents to bring

  • SSC certificate / transcript
  • HSC certificate / transcript
  • Diploma certificate — for the BTEB engineering diploma route
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Previous transcripts — if claiming transfer credits
  • For international applicants: passport, visa / travel documents and financial certification

Always confirm the exact list for your programme with the Admission Office before travelling to campus.

Graduate programmes

  • M.A. in English — 1 year, or Preliminary & Final
  • MBA — 1 year · Executive 1.5 years · Regular 2 years
  • LL.M — 1 year or 2 years

Some programmes shortlist candidates with a written test or an interview. International applicants are selected on academic work, proficiency in English and Mathematics, probability of success in the curriculum, and certification of adequate financial resources.

Transfer credit: credits earned at previously attended colleges and universities are evaluated for acceptance by faculty members — submit your transcripts for evaluation.

Still have a question about admission? Use the Admission Query Form, call the PABX, or ask the assistant — it answers from the university’s own admission rules.

Laboratories & resource centres

SIU’s dedicated development environment, used mainly by Computer Science & Engineering and Computer Science & Informatics students for practical programming, algorithms and software engineering work. It complements the general-purpose Computer Lab, the Browsing Lab and the Research Lab. Access follows your department’s lab schedule, and outside class hours for project work.
The central Computer Lab supports practical classes and project work across all departments — programming for CSE and CSI, electronics design work for ECE, statistical analysis for Business Administration, and legal research work for Law.

Two laboratories serve the Electronics & Communication Engineering department: the Electronics Lab for circuit analysis, devices and analog/digital electronics experiments, and the Electronics & Machine Lab for machines, motors, control and hardware experiments.
The Physics Lab supports the physical-science component of the science and engineering curricula, particularly for ECE students, who use it for foundational physics experiments alongside their electronics coursework.
SIU’s dedicated computing and training facility: additional computing access beyond the departmental laboratories, training and workshops in computer skills and ICT tools, and support for students who need extra practice time.

Academics

How study works at SIU

Syllabus

Each department publishes its own syllabus covering core and elective courses, credits and the semester-wise distribution.

Academic Calendar

Semester start and end dates, registration windows, midterm and submission deadlines, examinations, results and holidays.

Grading System

The 4.0 grade-point scale, reported as CGPA on a 4.0 basis alongside the GPA-5 and GPA-10 equivalents used for admission eligibility.

Examinations

Conducted by the Office of the Controller of Examinations — continuous assessment plus end-of-term written papers, with re-sits arranged through the same office.

How your CGPA works

  1. 1Each course carries credits, and your grade point for that course is multiplied by the credits to give credit points.
  2. 2CGPA = total credit points earned ÷ total credit attempted, rounded to two decimals.
  3. 3Waiver thresholds are quoted both ways — for example a diploma CGPA of 3.25 (out of 4.0) earns a 40% tuition waiver, while SSC + HSC totals use the 5.0 / 10.0 scale.

Medium of instruction

The medium of instruction at Sylhet International University is English — across all undergraduate and graduate programmes. International applicants should note that proficiency in English, along with Mathematics, is one of the criteria used in selection.
